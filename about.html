About Tim

Meet Tim Price

For more than 20 years, I’ve worked with leaders, teams and organisations to create meaningful change.

I believe the strongest leaders are those who are willing to reflect, learn and grow - and I’m here to help you do exactly that.

My story

Leadership is about people.

Over the past 20 years, I've had the privilege of working with executive leaders, emerging talent and teams across a wide range of organisations. My approach is built on trust, curiosity and meaningful conversations that create lasting change.

I support executive leaders who may have hit a bumb in the road or are looking to develop their leadership further. I help them identify their goals and action plans to reach them.

I also coach up-and-coming talent in organisations and help them prepare for the next level of their leadership.

I coach teams at all levels of organisations who want to connect at a more human level to produce the desired results through experimental workshops using tools such as Liberating Structures and Lego® Serious Play®.

I also run experimental leadership programmes to help leaders step into their authentic selves and explore what is possible when they co-create, co-lead, understand their own value and ask for help.

I’m also a certified Leadership Circle Profile® practitioner and use this in my coaching of both individuals and teams.
